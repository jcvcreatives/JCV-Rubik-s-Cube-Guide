Solving Instructions & Notation

Hold your cube with the GREEN center in front (F) and WHITE center on top (U). Keep this orientation fixed for every move—only twist the indicated outer layer.

7-Stage Manual Method

01. White Top Cross F2 · R2 · B2 · L2

Align the 4 White edges around the White (U) center and match their side centers.

02. First Layer Corners R' D' R D

Position a White corner below its target slot and repeat R' D' R D until White faces up.

03. Middle Layer Edges D' R' D R D F D' F'

Align a non-Yellow bottom edge with its side center and insert Right or Left.

04. Bottom Yellow Cross F D R D' R' F'

Advance the bottom Yellow pattern from Dot → L-Shape → Line → Cross.

05. Align Yellow Edges R D R' D R D2 R'

Hold two matched bottom edges at Back and Right, then execute Sune.

06. Position Final Corners D R D' L' D R' D' L

Cycle the 4 bottom corners into their matching 3-color corner positions.

07. Orient Final Corners (R U R' U') × 2 + D

Twist each remaining corner at Front-Right-Bottom until Yellow faces down.
